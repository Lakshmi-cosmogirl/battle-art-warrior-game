How to Play

Game Instructions

Objective: Reduce all 3 of the boss's weak points to 0%. The boss survives its first defeat and enrages with restored, tougher weak points — defeat it a second time to win the level and claim the treasure. Clear all 20 levels to complete the Campaign.

Movement & Dodging

  • WASD Move around the arena (W/S also shifts depth — step closer to hit harder, step back to dodge easier).
  • SPACE Dash-dodge, with brief invulnerability. Has a short cooldown shown on the stamina bar.

Weak Point Targeting

  • TAB or the on-screen Target button opens a radial menu and briefly slows time so you can pick a weak point. 123 also work instantly.
  • The Zoom button in the weak-point panel pushes the camera in on the boss for precision play on its larger, later-level forms.

Combat

  • Normal Attack (E) — quick strike on the targeted weak point.
  • Charged Attack (hold F) — costs Aura, hits much harder.
  • Special Skill (Q) — every warrior class has its own. Has a cooldown.
  • Ultimate (R) — unlocks only at full Aura. Unleashes an elemental cutscene finisher in your chosen aura color.
  • Land hits without getting struck to build a Combo multiplier, boosting damage. Getting hit resets it.

Warrior Classes & Special Skills

  • Kay — Guard Break: shatters the boss guard, stunning it and opening a bonus-damage window.
  • Yui — Blink Step: flash-steps to the boss with 1.2s invulnerability and an instant dodge reset.
  • Miyo — Aura Surge: grants +45 Aura instantly and stalls the boss mid-move.
  • Takashi — Iron Bulwark: 3 seconds of full immunity plus 15 HP mended.
  • Zuhu — Blood Frenzy: spikes the combo multiplier and doubles swing speed for 5 seconds.

The Boss

  • Bosses move, dash, and unleash telegraphed area and beam attacks — watch the warning flash and dodge.
  • Higher levels add new boss tactics: dash charges, ground slams, phase-guard invulnerability windows, and double beams.

Survival & Records

  • Potions heal 10 HP each; you carry more as levels increase.
  • 3 lives per run — losing all HP costs a life and respawns you; losing all 3 lives ends the run.
  • Battle history, high scores, and your Legendary Treasure inventory are saved automatically and viewable from Records.

Desktop Controls

  • WASD — Move
  • SPACE — Dodge
  • E — Attack
  • F — Charged Attack (hold)
  • Q — Special Skill
  • R — Ultimate
  • Z — Zoom on Boss (Potion has no hotkey — click the on-screen Potion button)
  • 123 — Target
  • TAB — Cycle Target
  • ESC — Close the target radial menu (there is no separate pause menu)

Mobile Controls (landscape)

  • Left side — the console: analog stick to move, Dodge button just above it.
  • Right side — the actions: Target, Attack, Charged, your class Skill, Ultimate and Potion.
  • Boss weak points and your HP / Aura bars sit in their own reserved lanes so no button is ever covered.
  • Hold your phone sideways — a prompt appears if you are upright.
